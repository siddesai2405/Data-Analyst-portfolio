📦 Inventory & Purchase Analysis
  📊 Data analysis project to understand inventory, purchasing, vendor performance, and product costs using Python, Pandas, SQL, and visualization.

🎯 Objectives
  📦 Analyze inventory levels and movement
  🛒 Analyze purchasing patterns
  🏪 Understand vendor contribution
  💰 Analyze product purchase prices
  📊 Generate business insights for better decisions
