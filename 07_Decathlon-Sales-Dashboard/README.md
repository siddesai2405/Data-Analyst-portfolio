Decathlon Sales Dashboard



A sales analytics project focused on presenting business performance through a dashboard, supported by sales data, product data, store data, and business requirements.



Tools \& Files

\- Excel: Sales, products, and store datasets

\- Dashboard: `Dashboard.png`

\- Business Requirements: `Decathlon-Dashboard-Business-Requirements.pdf`

\- Presentation: `Knowledge.pptx`

\- Supporting Assets: Logos, filter images, reset-filter icons, and KPI icons



Project Files

\- `sales.xlsx` — Sales dataset

\- `products.xlsx` — Product dataset

\- `store.xlsx` — Store dataset

\- `Dashboard.png` — Dashboard preview

\- `Decathlon-Dashboard-Business-Requirements.pdf` — Business requirements

\- `Knowledge.pptx` — Supporting presentation

\- `kpi-icons/` — KPI icon assets



Objective

Organize sales-related data and supporting business requirements into a dashboard project to help users explore business performance.



How to Explore

1\. Review the business requirements PDF.

2\. Open the Excel files to inspect the datasets.

3\. View `Dashboard.png` to preview the dashboard.

4\. Review `Knowledge.pptx` for supporting project information.
